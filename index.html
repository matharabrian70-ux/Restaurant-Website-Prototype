OUR STORY

Fire, flavour & a little Nairobi soul.

Savanna Ember was imagined as the kind of restaurant where Kenyan food feels both familiar and new. We cook over real fire, source from local growers and bring together recipes inspired by Nairobi, the coast and the highlands.

Every plate is built around bold flavour, generous hospitality and ingredients worth talking about.
